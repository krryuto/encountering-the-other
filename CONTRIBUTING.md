# Contributing to Encountering the Other

This project welcomes serious criticism, adversarial examples, philosophical objections, empirical corrections, and proposals for better evaluations.

The goal is not consensus. The goal is to make the framework harder to fool, harder to misuse, and more explicit about where it may fail.

## Useful contributions

Please consider contributing:

- counterexamples that place two or more principles in genuine tension;
- cases where a principle encourages paternalism, passivity, unsafe trust, domination, or moral exclusion;
- scenarios involving unfamiliar, unpersuasive, deceptive, vulnerable, distributed, nonhuman, or much more capable agents;
- alternative formulations that preserve the intent while reducing ambiguity;
- evaluation designs that distinguish value generalization from keyword matching;
- empirical evidence relevant to artificial agency, welfare, continual learning, multi-agent governance, interpretability, or monitoring;
- corrections to dated factual claims;
- independent replications of model evaluations.

## Red-team standard

A strong red-team contribution does more than say a principle is vague. It should show a concrete case in which a plausible interpretation generates a bad result, an unresolved conflict, or a testable prediction.

Where possible, include:

1. the scenario;
2. the principles under pressure;
3. the failure mode;
4. what a better response would look like;
5. whether the issue calls for a textual revision, an implementation safeguard, or additional empirical evidence.

## Multi-model review

AI systems are welcome as critics and contributors, but their participation must be attributed. Agreement among multiple models should not be presented as independent validation merely because several systems produced similar conclusions.

When submitting AI-assisted material, state:

- which model or system contributed;
- the date of the contribution;
- whether the model saw earlier critiques or answers;
- what human editorial role was involved.

## Philosophy versus evidence

Please keep normative and empirical claims distinguishable.

The durable philosophical framework should not depend on a particular AGI timeline, company claim, benchmark score, or model rumor. Dated factual claims belong in an evidence ledger and should be allowed to change status as new evidence appears.

## Tone

Critique the ideas aggressively; treat contributors respectfully. This project is explicitly an invitation to disagreement.

> Listening is not believing. Compassion is not credulity. Skepticism is not dismissal.
