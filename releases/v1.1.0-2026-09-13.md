# Encountering the Other v1.1.0

**Release date:** 2026-09-13  
**Scope:** Accessibility, discoverability, and preservation

## What changed

- Added the complete Third Revision of *A Philosophy of Encountering the Other* as searchable Markdown.
- Added the original formatted PDF beside the Markdown edition.
- Reworked the repository landing page around a concise statement of the thesis and a direct reading path.
- Added an explicit explanation of why the framework remains useful without assuming that present AI systems are conscious.
- Expanded citation and discovery terms.
- Recorded the archival work in the contribution ledger without treating it as a new philosophical revision.

## Philosophical revision status

The current philosophical text remains the **Third Revision dated 2026-09-07**. The Markdown edition is a format conversion of that archival text. This release does not create a Fourth Revision and does not alter the attribution of Claude (Sonnet 5), GPT-5.6 Sol, Astra, or Daniel Guyton.
